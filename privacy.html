Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Applies to HermesAgent (personal, single-user application)

Short version: HermesAgent is a personal assistant that I (its author and only user) run against my own Google Account using the official Google APIs. Data from Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, and Google Sheets is processed on my private server solely to carry out tasks I ask the assistant to perform. Nothing is sold, nothing is used for advertising, and access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers HermesAgent, a personal, single-user application. It is operated by its author for his own Google Account only and is not offered to, or usable by, any other person. This page is published as required by the Google OAuth verification process.

2. Google data the agent accesses

When granted authorization, the agent may access, depending on the task at hand:

  • Gmail — messages and attachments, to read, search, summarize, organize, and send email on request.
  • Google Drive — file metadata and content, to open, read, organize, and create documents and files on request.
  • Google Calendar — events, to check schedules, create, update, and manage calendar entries on request.
  • Google Sheets — spreadsheet data, to read, edit, and organize sheets on request.

The agent accesses only the scopes granted through a standard Google OAuth 2.0 consent screen, and only for the account of its owner.

3. How the data is used

Content from the connected Google services is used exclusively to perform the tasks that its owner requests — for example, summarizing an email thread, drafting a reply, creating an event, locating a document, or updating a spreadsheet. The application's purpose is task automation for a personal account; it does not build user profiles, target advertising, or market to anyone.

4. Hosting and processing

The agent runs on a single-tenant virtual private server operated by its author and hosted with Hostinger. OAuth access tokens for the agent's own account are stored on that server and are used only to connect to the relevant Google services. Content retrieved from Google services is processed there to complete requested tasks.

5. Third-party services

To produce its responses, the assistant may transmit the content it is working on (for example, an email being summarized or a document being drafted) to third-party machine learning services used by the application to interpret requests and generate output. Such content is used solely in service of the owner's task. No Google data is sold, rented, or shared with any party for advertising purposes.

6. What we do not do

  • I do not sell or rent Google data or any personal information.
  • Data is never used for advertising or to train commercial products.
  • This application does not serve other users and processes only the owner's own account.

7. Security

Access to Google services happens over encrypted connections (TLS) using official Google APIs. Access to the private server described in section 4 is restricted to its owner. The server and the agent's software are kept updated with publicly available security fixes.

8. Access revocation

Authorization can be revoked at any time without contacting the author: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove HermesAgent. Revocation permanently ends the application's ability to read or act on the connected Google services.

9. Data requests

Because this application serves a single user (its author), there is no third-party data to delete. If you nonetheless have a question about this policy or the agent's handling of Google data, contact the author through github.com/Saytor20.

10. Changes to this policy

If the agent's data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe them before they take effect, and the revised policy will be published on this page.
