HermesAgent

HermesAgent is a personal AI assistant operated by its own author for his own Google Account. It is not a commercial product and is not available to other users.

The assistant connects to four Google services via the official Google APIs and OAuth 2.0: it performs everyday tasks on its owner's behalf, such as reading and organizing email, drafting replies, keeping Calendar in order, arranging Drive files, and updating spreadsheets — only when the owner asks it to.

  • GmailRead, search, draft & send mail on request
  • Google DriveFind, read & organize documents and files
  • Google CalendarSchedule, check & update events
  • Google SheetsRead, edit & organize spreadsheet data

Under your control

The agent takes actions only when instructed, works with a single authorized Google Account, and can be disconnected at any time from Google Account permissions.
